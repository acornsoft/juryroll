### 6.15 Michigan

Juror questionnaire answers are not disclosed without the chief circuit judge's order (MCL 600.1315). JuryRoll refuses any Michigan jury list.
The jury list comes from driver's license and ID records. Those records include lawful non-citizens.
The Bureau of Elections asks county clerks to send it possible non-citizens found through jury forms. The Bureau sends a notice. If the voter does not answer, the registration is cancelled. This is guidance from a March 11, 2026 memo, not a law.
The state voter file has year of birth and registration date, but not full date of birth. Matches stay capped.
A voter may challenge by affidavit (MCL 168.512). A baseless or harassing challenge is a misdemeanor. JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Ask the Bureau of Elections for counts: clerk referrals, notices sent and cancellations. Courts are not covered by Michigan FOIA. Ask for counts only.
