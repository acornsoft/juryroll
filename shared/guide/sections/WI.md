### 6.16 Wisconsin

Juror qualification forms are confidential. Only a court can release them, for good cause (Wis. Stat. 756.04(11)(a)). JuryRoll refuses any Wisconsin jury list.
The public jury array lists qualified jurors only. Names of people found not qualified are struck and discarded at the end of the jury year (756.04(9)).
No Wisconsin law sends juror non-citizen answers to election officials.
Date of birth in the voter list is for election staff only. Matches stay capped.
A voter may challenge another voter by affidavit (6.48). JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Ask the Elections Commission or the municipal clerk for counts of non-citizen cancellations. Ask for counts only.
