### 6.9 Kentucky

Each month the Administrative Office of the Courts lists people excused from jury duty as non-citizens. It sends the list to the Attorney General, the U.S. Attorney and the State Board of Elections (KRS 30A.145(2)). The State Board removes listed voters within 5 days (KRS 116.113(3)).
Jury selection records are confidential (AP Part II, Sec. 12). No law makes the list public. Do not ask for names.
Kentucky has no private challenge before an election. Challenges happen only at the polls (KRS 117.245). JuryRoll does not support them.
Only candidates, parties, ballot committees and people the State Board approves can buy the voter list (KRS 117.025(3)(i)). You must sign the SBE 84 form under oath.
You can ask the State Board for monthly counts: names received, removals and protests (KRS 61.872; 52 U.S.C. 20507(i)). Read the annual cleanup report for citizenship status change counts (KRS 116.114). Ask for counts only.
