### 6.10 Oklahoma

Each month the court clerk lists people excused from jury duty as non-citizens. The clerk gives the list to the county election board. The board cancels listed voters and reports them to the district attorney and the U.S. attorney (26 O.S. 4-120.5(B)).
Do not ask court clerks for the monthly lists under the Open Records Act. This approach was posted on July 31, 2026. It fails. Juror names are confidential outside open court unless a judge orders otherwise (38 O.S. 36(B)).
The State Election Board publishes canceled registrations with the reason (26 O.S. 4-120(C)). Reason code JD means "excused from jury duty (non-citizen)". JD counts show that the official pipeline already works.
Use the JD counts by county and month only. Do not match, keep, show or post the names in that file. They are finished official actions, not leads. JuryRoll skips every canceled row before matching.
Oklahoma has no private challenge law. JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
You can also ask the county election board for monthly counts: lists received, cancellations and referrals (52 U.S.C. 20507(i)). Ask for counts only.
