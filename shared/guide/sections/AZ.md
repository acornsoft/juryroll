### 6.4 Arizona

Arizona's non-juror lists are closed by state law (A.R.S. 21-312; Ariz. R. Sup. Ct. 123(e)(10)). Federal law does not close them. Don't request them.
You can ask the Secretary of State for its quarterly counts under A.R.S. 16-165(M)(3): jury-questionnaire non-citizen statements, notices and cancellations. Ask for counts only. Do not ask for names.
Do not challenge a voter at the polls because of JuryRoll output.
Arizona voter lists carry birth year only, not the full birth date. Do not post voter data on the internet. That is a felony (A.R.S. 16-168(F)).
Pending: the U.S. Supreme Court will hear RNC v. Mi Familia Vota (No. 25-1017) on December 8, 2026. Until it decides, the 90-day limit on Arizona cancellations stays in place.
