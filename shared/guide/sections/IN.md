### 6.13 Indiana

Every even year the state asks the federal courts for people disqualified from jury service as non-citizens. Counties get the list after the primary and send a notice (IC 3-7-38.2-16).
The master list of juror names is public, but all other jury information is confidential (IC 33-28-5-13(d)). A list of names is not a list of non-citizens. JuryRoll refuses any Indiana jury list.
A private requester gets a voter list without date of birth or registration date (IC 3-7-26.4-8). Matches stay capped, and the date check cannot run.
Indiana allows challenges only at the polls. JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Ask the county voter registration office for counts: jury records received, notices sent and cancellations. Ask for counts only. A 2026 law lets offices check where requesters live and charge non-residents more.
