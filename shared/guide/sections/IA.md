### 6.18 Iowa

Only a prospective juror's year of birth and city and zip code are public (Iowa Code 607A.48(1)). JuryRoll refuses any Iowa jury list.
A juror who is not a citizen may have to give the court documentation. That documentation cancels the voter registration (607A.4(5); 48A.30(1)(f)).
Voter lists may be used only for elections, political purposes, official purposes of elected officials, or political research. Commercial use is a crime (48A.39; 39A.3).
A voter may challenge another voter (48A.14). JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Ask the county auditor for counts of cancellations based on jury documentation. Also read the state registrar's yearly cancellation report (47.7(2)(f)). Ask for counts only.
