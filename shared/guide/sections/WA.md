### 6.7 Washington

The non-citizen answers jurors give the court are not public. Juror information other than the name is presumed private (GR 31(j)). The court may use qualification answers only for the jury term (RCW 2.36.072(4)).
No Washington law sends juror non-citizen answers to election officials. Do not ask a court for a list.
A registered voter may challenge another voter only with personal knowledge (RCW 29A.08.810). A JuryRoll match is not personal knowledge. Do not file a challenge from a match.
Do not file a challenge within 45 days of an election. Do not challenge an inactive voter (RCW 29A.08.810(4), 2026 c 212).
The public voter file shows year of birth only. JuryRoll matches stay at Probable or lower.
You can ask the county auditor for counts of citizenship challenges and their outcomes (RCW 42.56.070; 52 U.S.C. 20507(i)). Ask for counts only. Do not ask for names.
