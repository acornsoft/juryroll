### 6.6 California

California has no law that sends juror non-citizen answers to election officials. SB 1310 (2026) would have done this. It failed in committee.
No named list of non-citizen jurors is public. Counts of jury disqualifications are not names. They do not show who is registered.
Only precinct board members and election officials may challenge a voter (Elec. Code 14240(b)). Do not send lists to poll workers.
You can get the voter file only for a purpose the Secretary of State approves, under penalty of perjury (2188, 2194). Do not post it on the internet. Misuse is a crime (18109).
You can ask the county elections official for counts of non-citizen cancellations (Gov. Code 7922.525; 52 U.S.C. 20507(i)). Ask for counts only. Do not ask for names.
