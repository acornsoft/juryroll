### 6.19 Connecticut

Lawful permanent residents can serve on Connecticut juries (Conn. Gen. Stat. 51-217(a)). A Connecticut jury record is not evidence of unlawful registration.
Juror questionnaires are not public records (51-232(c)(1)). JuryRoll refuses any Connecticut jury list.
The list of qualified jurors goes to the Secretary of the State to check online registrations (9-19l). That list includes permanent residents.
Challenges happen only at the polls, and citizenship is not a ground (9-232). JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Counts only. Any count carries the label "not evidence of unlawful registration in this jurisdiction".
