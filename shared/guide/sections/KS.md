### 6.12 Kansas

A jury commissioner sends citizenship information about disqualified jurors to the Secretary of State. The law limits its use to voter registration upkeep (K.S.A. 43-174).
No law makes this information public. JuryRoll refuses any Kansas jury list.
Commercial use of the voter list is a crime (K.S.A. 25-2320a). JuryRoll does not know if the state file has a date of birth, so matches stay capped.
Kansas has no private challenge law. JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Ask the Secretary of State for counts by county and month: jury reports received and removals. Also read the annual SAVE Kansas report (due January 31). Ask for counts only.
