### 6.5 Tennessee

Tennessee posts the jury pool list for public inspection (T.C.A. 22-2-308). That list has names only. It does not show citizenship. A match against it is not a lead.
The list of jurors disqualified as non-citizens goes to election officials (2-2-113). No law makes it public. Don't request it.
You can ask the county election commission for counts of non-citizen notices and purges (2-2-141; 10-7-503; 52 U.S.C. 20507(i)). Ask for counts only. Do not ask for names.
You must certify that a voter list is for political purposes (2-2-138). A false certification is a crime. If you are not sure your use fits, ask your county election commission.
Challenges happen only at the polls (2-7-123). Do not challenge a voter because of JuryRoll output.
