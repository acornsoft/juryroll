### 6.8 Georgia

Each superior court clerk sends the Secretary of State a monthly list of people who say at jury qualification that they are not citizens (O.C.G.A. 21-2-231(a.1)). The county registrars remove listed voters and mail a notice (21-2-231(c)(1)).
No law makes this list public. Jury list data held by the clerks' council is exempt from the Open Records Act (50-18-72(a)(6)). Do not ask the clerk or the Secretary of State for names.
Any elector may challenge a voter in the same county (21-2-229, 21-2-230). The challenger must prove the case. A JuryRoll match alone is not enough. JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance in Georgia, because no lawful jury list exists.
A challenge filed within 45 days of an election waits until certification (21-2-230(b)(1)).
The public voter list has no full date of birth. JuryRoll matches stay at Probable or lower.
You can ask the Secretary of State or the county registrars for monthly counts: names on the clerk lists, names sent to registrars, removals and notices (50-18-71; 52 U.S.C. 20507(i)). Ask for counts only.
