### 6.11 Louisiana

Each month the clerk of court tells the Department of State about jury notices that show a person is not a citizen. The Department sends them to the parish registrar (R.S. 18:178(B)).
Do not ask a parish clerk for a "non-juror list". No law makes it public, and a 2026 law makes a juror's identity not a public record (C.Cr.P. art. 794.1). JuryRoll refuses any Louisiana jury list.
The registrar mails a notice and gives 21 days to show cause before cancellation (R.S. 18:193(G)). Those notice lists name people. They are official actions, not leads. Do not use them.
The purchased voter list has a registration date but no date of birth. The app can run the date check.
Louisiana has no private challenge before an election. JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Ask the Secretary of State or the registrar for counts by parish and month: jury notices received, notices mailed and cancellations. Also read the annual report on non-citizen cancellations (R.S. 18:49.2(E)). Ask for counts only.
