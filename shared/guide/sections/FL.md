### 6.3 Florida

Florida keeps no non-citizen jury list. The state's jury source list holds only citizens. Don't ask the clerk for one.
You can ask the Department of State or the county supervisor of elections for the supervisor's twice-yearly list-maintenance certification (Fla. Stat. 98.075(8), 119.07(1); 52 U.S.C. 20507(i)).
Ask for counts and outcomes only. Do not ask for names.
A challenge under Fla. Stat. 101.111 is about one vote at one election. A JuryRoll match is not enough for the sworn "reason to believe". A false oath is a felony (104.011(1)).
Dated note (2026-10-07): 98.093(9) and the 98.075(6)(a) step take effect January 1, 2027 (ch. 2026-26). They are not current law.
