### 6.17 West Virginia

The court decides who is disqualified, including non-citizens. The clerk writes the decision on the juror form and on the lists of drawn names (W. Va. Code 52-1-8(a)).
Under review. The drawn-names lists may carry a 'not a citizen' entry, but no court or AG ruling settles whether they're open under WV FOIA's privacy exemption. JuryRoll won't load them until that's resolved.
No statute says the drawn lists carry a date of birth or address. They are "alphabetical lists of names" (52-1-8(a)). So every match would be name-only anyway, and name-only matches are never exported.
A voter may challenge in person at the county clerk (3-2-28). If the voter does not appear within 30 days, the registration is cancelled. JuryRoll gives no challenge guidance.
Ask the circuit clerk for counts of jurors disqualified as non-citizens, and the county clerk for counts of non-citizen cancellations. Ask for counts only.
